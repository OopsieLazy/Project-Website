CAD's Creations

There is an old idiom that has had a hold of my heart for years: "Create something from nothing". This beautifully represents why I started my journey in CAD. To create for the sake of creating, to let imagination run wild. An idea one second could become something real in an instant. I began about 7 years ago at the start of my university adventures when my love for CAD was small, not a passion but it steadily grew. It is a blast from start to finish, and when you're done you get to actually hold your hard work. So here are a few of the designs, parts, and projects I've made over the years!
