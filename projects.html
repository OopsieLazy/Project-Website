    CAD's Creations

    There is an old idiom that has had a hold of my heart for years: "Create something from nothing". This beautifully represents why I started my journey in CAD. To create for the sake of creating, to let imagination run wild. An idea one second could become something real in an instant. I began about 7 years ago at the start of my university adventures when my love for CAD was small, not a passion but it steadily grew.
