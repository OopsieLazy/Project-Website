Background

From 2021 to 2024 Zach led the steering design for the University of Ottawa's GNCTR concrete toboggan team: three seasons of rebuilding the system after the team's COVID pause, revising it from test results, and machining and welding the parts on CNC, lathe and shop equipment. Summers as a junior inspector with WSP meant checking installed sewer and water services against engineering drawings and standards.

Since January 2026 Zach has run a small product design business, with 13 original designs published on maker platforms and 8 products for sale: laboratory accessories, portable games, workshop tools and hobby gear. A small-engine repair service in Clarington runs alongside it.

Education

Zach studied Mechanical Engineering (B.A.Sc. program) at the University of Ottawa from 2020 to 2024 and plans to complete the remaining requirements. Highlights include first place at the Design Day competition with a medical VR program, and two roles in the Engineering Students Society: first-year representative and manager of sports.
